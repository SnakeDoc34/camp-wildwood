# Camp Wildwood: next steps

Updated October 12, 2026. Keep Camp Wildwood separate from the Veridia game.
The public game currently teaches the 12 implemented skills of Chapters 8–9.
The chapter names below describe the existing roadmap, not completed lessons.

## Stage 1 — Make the camp worth building

The `camp-build1` release adds a camp designer, meaningful shop purchases,
saved layouts, a savings goal, mission reward previews, and a soccer activity.
The first purchase goal is a four-token soccer kit. Children can arrange six
places around their lodge and meadow. Free welcome items allow immediate play.

The learning loop is: learn a strategy, finish practice, earn a token, choose an
item, arrange it, and enjoy the changed camp. Completed practice replays earn
another token; the first Adventure challenge for each skill adds one bonus.
Hints support learning and never cost tokens. Camp play is always available
and does not create extra tokens. There are no streak penalties or timers.

Acceptance: legacy saves and purchases retained, purchases charged once,
layouts isolated by camper and retained after refresh, duplicate completions
do not pay twice, soccer score and token rules verified, usable native controls,
and a physical iPad Safari trial. The device trial remains outstanding.

## Stage 2 — Trading Post and Picnic Grove

| Planned chapter | Camp destination | Proposed useful activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10: Money | Trading Post | Arrange a stall, choose its sign, and use a pretend checkout with amounts tied to reviewed lessons. |
| 11: Fractions | Picnic Grove | Arrange picnic furniture and share pictured food in mathematically exact equal parts. |

Before building either chapter, map its targets, vocabulary, models, and example
structures against the publisher's guide and the parent-supplied 2B materials.
Confirm the expected currency and scope for Money. Do not introduce a new
procedure through a shop hint before it has been taught.

Build and release one chapter at a time. Use the same demonstration → controls →
guided work → independent practice → optional Adventure structure. First audit
the explanations and diagrams; then test generated examples, wrong answers,
hints, saved work, rewards, and actual iPad controls. Only then turn the trail
into a playable chapter. Make its destination visibly useful in the camp.

## Stage 3 — Expand the camp and hold the festival

| Planned chapter | Destination | Proposed activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12: Time | Adventure Station | Set a pictured clock and arrange a camp-day schedule. |
| 13: Capacity | Water Works | Fill and compare containers using reviewed units and exact quantities. |
| 14: Graphs | Fishing Dock | Collect pictured catches and build a graph that matches the data exactly. |
| 15: Shapes | Woodland Workshop | Arrange structures with reviewed shape properties. |
| Finale | Camp Festival | Display the child's camp and completed trails, with short review games. |

These are design proposals. Validate the curriculum scope before committing
specific lessons, units, difficulty, or game rules. Decorative scenery must never
substitute for a mathematical diagram or imply an incorrect quantity.

## Cross-cutting follow-up

- Test physical iPad Safari: portrait/landscape, taps, keyboard, narration, and
  refresh. Current browser verification is desktop Chrome.
- Record matching narration for revised lessons if the parent prefers the
  existing recorded voice to on-device speech.
- Add a reversible export/import backup before any cloud sync work. Current
  profiles, purchases, and camp layouts are local to each browser and device.
- Consider larger field layouts and more camp activities after children have
  tried the six-place builder. Avoid expanding the shop faster than useful play.
- Decide whether offline support is needed; this release has no service worker.

For reliable continuation, keep each chapter and each reward feature as a tested
checkpoint with a source commit and QA note. The project can progress in three
stages without trying to implement all remaining chapters in one run.
